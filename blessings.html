28 Blessings

The Blessings of Faith

"For whatsoever is born of God overcometh the world: and this is the victory that overcometh the world, even our faith." 1 John 5:4

What Faith Unlocks

Twenty-eight ways faith reshapes a life.

If the Promises are what God has said He will do, the Blessings are what actually happens in a life that takes Him at His word — healing, provision, righteousness, boldness, and more. They're grouped below into four themes so you can explore at your own pace rather than scrolling through all twenty-eight at once.

Reflect

Which blessing feels distant right now?

Not every blessing feels active in every season — that's normal, not a sign your faith has failed. Pick one from the list that feels far away today, and ask what it would look like to simply receive it rather than earn it.

A Prayer

Lord, thank You that faith isn't just belief — it's an open hand. Let me receive what You've already provided instead of striving for what You've already given. Where I feel weak, let Your strength be made perfect. Where I feel far from You, draw near, as You've promised You would. Amen.
