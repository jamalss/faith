10 Promises

The Promises of Faith

Ten commitments God has made to those who trust Him — spoken by Christ, confirmed by the apostles, and still standing today.

Why Promises Matter

A promise is only as strong as the one who made it.

Every promise below rests on the character of God, not on circumstance. That's what separates a biblical promise from wishful thinking — it isn't a guess about what might happen, it's a commitment from someone who has never broken His word. Read slowly. Let each one sit before moving to the next.

Reflect

Which promise do you need today?

Faith isn't pretending you don't have questions — it's bringing those questions to a God who has already answered them in His Word. Before you move on, ask yourself honestly: which one of these ten do I have the hardest time believing? Sit with that one a little longer.

A Prayer

Lord, I want to trust Your promises even when what I see says otherwise. Where my faith is small, grow it. Where I've given up on a promise, remind me that Your word doesn't expire. Teach me to wait on You the way these promises ask me to. Amen.
