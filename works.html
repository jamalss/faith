17 Works

The Works of Faith

"Who through faith subdued kingdoms, wrought righteousness, obtained promises, stopped the mouths of lions…" Hebrews 11:33-38

Faith in Motion

These aren't metaphors. They happened.

Hebrews 11 doesn't describe faith in the abstract — it names names. Real people, real danger, real deliverance. Below are the seventeen works that chapter points to, in the order the chapter gives them: from kingdoms subdued to wanderings endured. Some are triumphant. Some end in suffering that faith carried through rather than escaped. Both count.

Reflect

Triumph and endurance are both faith.

Notice how many of these works aren't rescues — they're people who kept trusting God through the suffering rather than out of it. If your faith right now feels more like endurance than victory, you're still in good company on this list.

A Prayer

Lord, give me the kind of faith that acts — not faith that only agrees with You in theory. And when my faith is tested instead of rewarded, let me be counted among those who held on anyway, trusting You with what I cannot yet see resolved. Amen.
