What Is Faith

Not a feeling you conjure.
A trust you're invited into.

Scripture describes faith as confidence in what we hope for and assurance about what we cannot yet see. It isn't blind optimism, and it isn't naïve. It's a settled trust, built one promise at a time, that God is who He says He is — even when life says otherwise.

This site exists to help you build that trust: through the promises God has made, the blessings faith unlocks, the works faith produces, and the lives of those who walked this road before you.
